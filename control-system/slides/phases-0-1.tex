\begin{frame}{\texttt{control-system.ino}}
    \large
    We provide to you a complete sketch of a basic control system, \texttt{control-system.ino}
    \vspace{1em}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item reads hall effect sensors to compute speeds 
        \item decides how much power to send to the motor
        \item outputs to LEDs and the serial montior
    \end{itemize}
    \vspace{1em}
    It works on both the mini rig (Autumn semester) and the big rigs (Spring Semester).

    \vspace{1em}
    Upload it to the arduino once, at the start of phase 1. No need to change it until Phase 2.
\end{frame}
